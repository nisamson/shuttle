# SHL Rules

These are the rules for line and tactical deployments in the Simulation Hockey League.

## Lines

- In all lines, all positions must be filled.
- For even-strength lines:
  - Lines may not repeat players. If a player is on the first line, for example, they may not be on the second, third, or fourth.
  - Forward positions must be filled with forwards and defense positions must be filled with defensemen.

## Roles

Lineup role assignments are restricted by the following limits. There may be no more than:
- 1 enforcer or goon. (clarification needed: is that a combined limit of 1 for both roles, or 1 for each role?)
- 1 agitator.
- 1 crease-clearing defenseman.
- 1 shadow.
- 3 players per role, except for the above limits.

## Tactics

Teams may set tactical tendencies or settings only at the global level. Per-unit or per-player tactical settings are prohibited.

# SMJHL Rules

These are the rules for line and tactical deployments in the Simulation Major Junior Hockey League.

The rules for lines and roles are the same as for the SHL, with the following exceptions:

## Lines
- All even strength lines must have their TOI set to a minimum of 5 minutes.
- Forwards and defensemen may be assigned to any position on the line and may use roles of their assigned position.
- A player may be assigned only once to a line in each category (even strength, power play, penalty kill). For example, a player may be assigned to the first line on even strength and the first line on the power play, but not to the second line on even strength or the second line on the power play.
